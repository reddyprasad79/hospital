Seamless Cashless Hospitalization Support

Our 24/7 dedicated Corporate & TPA Desk coordinates directly with over 50 leading health insurance providers and Third-Party Administrators (TPAs) to secure fast pre-authorizations and seamless claim settlements.

1

Desk Intimation

Present your health card, policy copy & government ID at the TPA Desk.

2

Pre-Auth Filing

Treating doctor fills clinical diagnosis and estimated surgery costs for submission.

3

Approval Sanction

Insurance company reviews and grants initial approval within 45 to 60 minutes.

4

Discharge & Settle

Direct hospital-to-insurer bill clearance. Patient only settles non-medical consumables.

Tamil Nadu CMCHIS Scheme

Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme beneficiaries can avail cashless treatment for listed surgical procedures, cardiac surgeries, and cancer chemotherapy.

Valid CMCHIS Smart Card Required

Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)

Empaneled under Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana providing cashless coverage up to ₹5 Lakhs per eligible family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalizations.

Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY Card Required
Cashless Checklist

Documents to Bring for Cashless Pre-Approval

  • Health Insurance E-Card / Physical Card & Policy Number
  • Patient Government Photo ID (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Passport)
  • Prior medical history, prescription notes, and diagnostic test reports
  • Corporate Employee ID card (if claiming corporate group coverage)
