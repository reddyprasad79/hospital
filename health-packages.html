Preparation Tips

How to Prepare for Your Health Screening

  • 10-12 Hours Fasting: Do not consume food or caffeinated beverages overnight. Plain water is permitted.
  • Early Morning Arrival: Report between 7:30 AM and 8:30 AM at the Preventive Care Executive Lounge for blood and urine sampling.
  • Complimentary Breakfast: A dietitian-curated wholesome breakfast will be served after fasting blood samples.
  • Same-Day Physician Review: Comprehensive doctor consultation with all lab test reports explained by 2:00 PM.
